Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tool completely free to use?
Yes, our passport photo maker is 100% free with no hidden charges, watermarks, or registration required. All processing happens in your browser.
What image formats are supported?
We support JPG, PNG, and WebP formats. You can upload photos from your camera, phone, or any digital source.
Can I use this tool on mobile devices?
Absolutely! Our tool is fully responsive and optimized for mobile browsers. You can upload photos directly from your phone's gallery.
What are the passport photo requirements?
For Indian passports: 35x45mm with white background. For US passports: 2x2 inches (51x51mm) with white or off-white background. Our presets automatically apply these dimensions.
Is my photo data secure?
Yes, all image processing happens locally in your browser. We never upload or store your photos on any server. Your privacy is completely protected.
